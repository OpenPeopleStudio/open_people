# SSO/SCIM Lighthouse Partner Brief

> Owner: Sam
> Date: 2026-01-22
> Audience: CEO, CTO

## Objective
Establish an enterprise lighthouse for SSO/SCIM to validate demand, refine requirements, and open a repeatable partnership lane.

## Recommended Partner Order
1) Okta (primary lighthouse)
2) Google Workspace (secondary)
3) Azure AD / Entra (follow-up)
4) WorkOS (accelerator option)
5) JumpCloud / OneLogin / Ping (later)

## Why Okta First
- Highest enterprise demand and fastest co-sell leverage.
- Strong SAML + SCIM expectations align with current schema and roadmap.
- Great signal for enterprise readiness and procurement.

## Pilot Structure
- 1-2 lighthouse design partners.
- 4-6 week integration window.
- Success criteria: SSO login success > 99.9%, SCIM create/update/deprovision working, audit logs verified.

## Commercial Notes (Typical)
- Per-user/per-month pricing with enterprise minimums.
- SSO/SCIM often treated as premium add-ons.
- Expect partner directory listing and potential co-marketing after pilot.

## Risks
- Auth middleware gaps or inconsistent audit logging may slow enterprise validation.
- Tenant resolution edge cases when domains overlap.
- IdP misconfigurations requiring strong admin docs and support.

## Next Actions
- Draft tenant-scoped SSO model + role mapping defaults (in progress).
- Decide lighthouse target (Okta vs Google Workspace) by 2026-01-29.
- Line up 1-2 pilot customers and define success metrics.
