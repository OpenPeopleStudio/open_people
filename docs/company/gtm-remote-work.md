# Remote Work GTM Brief (2026 H1)

Owner: Sam (CTO)  
Date: 2026-01-22  
Audience: CEO, CTO, Sales

## Objective
Position OpenPeople as the governed AI operations layer for remote-first teams, anchored on identity + chat hubs + audit-ready workflows.

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
- Remote-first or hybrid teams (50-1000 seats).
- Regulated or security-conscious orgs that need audit trails.
- Existing stacks include Slack/Teams + Okta/Google Workspace.

## Core Narrative
"Bring governed AI where remote teams already work. OpenPeople connects identity, chat, and workflows with audit-ready controls."

## Packaging & Pricing (Draft)
- **Remote Work Core**: AI chat + knowledge + workflows + policy controls.
- **Enterprise Access** (add-on): SSO/SCIM + audit exports + advanced governance.
- **Channel Surface** (add-on): Slack/Teams bot + meeting summaries.

## Channels & Distribution
- **Marketplace distribution**: Slack + Teams app listings (phase 2).
- **Enterprise lighthouse**: Okta/Google Workspace co-sell pilots.
- **Content motion**: Remote-work governance playbooks + case studies.

## Sales Motion
- Land via security + compliance requirements (SSO/SCIM, audit trails).
- Expand with chat hub automation + meeting summaries.
- Prove ROI with workflow automation and decision-to-task conversion.

## Proof Points to Build
- Tenant-scoped audit trails for AI actions.
- Governance workflows (HITL + policy gating).
- Remote team adoption metrics (activity + ROI dashboard).

## Risks
- Identity integration delays slow enterprise conversion.
- Security review needed for any new external connectors.
- Slack/Teams MVP scope creep.

## Next Actions
1) Confirm lighthouse IdP target and pilot customers by 2026-01-29.
2) Scope Slack/Teams MVP and stage release sequence.
3) Define GTM metrics: activation, adoption, ROI, retention.
